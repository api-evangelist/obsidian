Obsidian 0.13 introduces a brand new editor engine that comes with several enhancements to your editing experience. In this post, we'll take a closer look at one of the biggest improvements in the latest release: Live Preview . Live Preview is a new editor mode that lets you preview your notes in the same view that you're writing them in.
