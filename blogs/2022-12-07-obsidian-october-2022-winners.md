Our second Obsidian October has come to a close, and once again we were overwhelmed with all the amazing content, plugins, and themes that the Obsidian community created. We received 84 entries , judged by our panel that included joethei, liam, luke, ryanjamurphy, bri, silver and kepano. In keeping with our “Back to School” theme, a few of the entries were selected by our judges as their 🧑‍🎓 student pick , ideal for anyone in school and academia.
